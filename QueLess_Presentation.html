01 · The Problem

Why Physical Queues Fail in the Modern Era

Four real-world pain points that demand a digital solution.

📉 Wasted Productive Time

Globally, billions of productive hours are lost each year standing in static physical lines with no visibility into when service will begin or how long it will take.

🧠 Uncertainty & Anxiety

Research shows uncertain and unoccupied waiting feels 3× longer than transparent waiting. Paper slip tokens offer zero progress feedback or reassurance.

🏥 Overcrowded Waiting Halls

Crowded lobbies in clinics and diagnostic labs are prime hotspots for cross-contamination. Patients and visitors are forced to sit in close proximity for extended periods.

🔥 Front-Desk Staff Burnout

Receptionists constantly field verbal status inquiries while manually tracking walk-ins. There is zero data on counter throughput, delay patterns, or peak-load bottlenecks.

02 · The Solution

Introducing QueLess — A Unified Queue Ecosystem

Three perfectly synchronised touchpoints replacing the entire physical queuing workflow.

📱 Customer Web App

  • Discover nearby businesses with live GPS distance and crowd-load meters.
  • Join a virtual queue remotely or book a calendar appointment slot.
  • Track status live: Waiting → Approaching → Now Serving.
  • Airline-style Digital Boarding Pass with scannable QR code.

🖥️ Staff Desk Console

  • JWT-authenticated workspace for desk operators and branch managers.
  • One-click: Call Next • Complete • No-Show • Hold / Skip.
  • 5-second walk-in ticket generator for non-smartphone visitors.
  • Historical analytics: service duration and daily throughput charts.

📺 Public Lobby TV Screen

  • Fullscreen signage mode at /display/:id for any TV browser.
  • High-contrast current-token cards with active counter badges.
  • Melodic chime via HTML5 Web Audio API (zero audio files needed).
  • Spoken voice announcement: "Token 42, Counter 2" via Web Speech API.
03 · How It Works

6-Stage Frictionless Journey

From remote discovery to QR-verified service completion — fully synchronised.

STEP 01

🔍 Discover & Select

Customer finds a clinic, bank, or salon. Views live GPS distance, hours, and current crowd load.

STEP 02

🎟️ Instant Virtual Token

System assigns atomic sequential token (e.g. A-42) and generates a digital QR Boarding Pass.

STEP 03

⏱️ Dynamic Smart ETA

Wait time calculated from queue depth, counter speed, and real-time GPS transit duration.

STEP 04

🔔 Approaching Alert

System auto-alerts customer when 2 tokens remain, prompting them to head to the counter.

STEP 05

🔊 Multi-Modal Call

Staff taps "Call Next". TV flashes token, melodic chime rings, and voice announces the token aloud.

STEP 06

✅ QR Verify & Close

Staff scans the digital QR pass, completes service, and visit duration logs to the analytics database.

04 · Syllabus Architecture & Engineering

React Architecture & Syllabus Alignment

Engineered to demonstrate mastery of multi-page SPA routing, state architecture, and form handling.

🧭 Multi-Page SPA Navigation (React Router v7)

15+ declarative client routes with zero page reloads. Includes role-based route guards (<ProtectedRoute />), dynamic params (:businessId), and standalone TV lobby kiosk mode.

🗄️ State Architecture & Context API

Strict separation of Global State (AuthContext, ThemeContext, ToastContext) vs Local State, plus a Zustand-style queueStore via React's useSyncExternalStore.

📝 Form Handling & Validation Strategies

Modular, accessible <FormField /> & <FormSelect /> components. Features client-side RFC email regex, phone verification, and live 4-tier password strength scoring.

⚡ Async State & Error Handling

<AsyncHandler /> with loading skeletons, retry triggers, and React 19 Error Boundary. Includes automatic zero-crash offline database fallback if MySQL is unreachable.
